The bundled browser distribution was modified for wa-scheduler:
- Its export is retained in a private closure rather than assigned to self.WPP.
- Its configuration is private rather than read from or written to window.WPPConfig.
- Google Analytics, optional status synchronization and device naming are disabled
  before initialization. External link-preview services are not configured.
- file-type's Node-only stream helper is disabled instead of evaluating require.
